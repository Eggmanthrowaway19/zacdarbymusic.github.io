About

Currently studying a Bachelor of Music at ANU, majoring in Composition.

My compositional interest sits mainly in mid-20th century jazz — I'm drawn to the harmonic language of Monk and the voicing sensibility of Ellington, and I'm working through genre-specific tools like odd-bar extensions, through-composed form, and the false return.

Outside the score, this is where the rest of what I make lives...
